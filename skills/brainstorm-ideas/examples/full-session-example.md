# Example: Brainstorming a Reading-Tracker App

**Scenario**: Greenfield idea — the user turns a vague app concept into an actionable plan. The session runs through all 8 phases in order.

**Applies**: **facilitate-session**
**Phases**: **clarify-idea**, **identify-goals**, **explore-challenges**, **generate-ideas**, **evaluate-ideas**, **challenge-ideas**, **refine-idea**, **compile-actionable-plan**

## Input / Context
User request: "I want to build a mobile app that helps people track their daily reading habits. Not sure where to start."

## Session Walkthrough

### 1. clarify-idea
Facilitator: "Let me restate: a mobile app for tracking daily reading. Correct me if I am off."
User: "Close — users log what they read each day and see progress over time."
Facilitator: "Who is it for, and what problem does it solve?"
User: "Casual readers who want to build a reading habit but keep forgetting to read."
Facilitator: "Are you starting fresh, or is there existing work?"
User: "Starting fresh, no code yet."
Facilitator: "Any assumption to test — for example, that people will log every day?"
User: "I assumed daily logging, but maybe that's too much friction."

**Confirmed idea**: A mobile app for casual readers to log reading and build a habit, keeping logging effort low.

### 2. identify-goals
Facilitator: "What do you want to achieve — for example, a usable product in 3 months, or learning a framework?"
User: "A usable product I can launch in 3 months, and I want to learn mobile development."
Facilitator: "How will you know it succeeded?"
User: "A closed beta with 20 engaged users who use it weekly."

**Confirmed goals**: launch in 3 months; learn mobile development; 20 weekly-active beta users.

### 3. explore-challenges
Facilitator: "What challenges do you foresee — for example, limited time or retention?"
User: "Weekend-only development time, and users may stop logging after a few weeks."
Facilitator: "What could cause the app to fail?"
User: "If logging feels like too much effort, people abandon it."
Facilitator: "Any constraints, such as budget or platform?"
User: "No budget for paid services; iOS first."

**Confirmed challenges**: weekend-only time; retention; logging friction; no paid services.

### 4. generate-ideas
Facilitator: "Here are 5–7 diverse ideas, generated by analogy, inversion, combination, and elimination, presented as a mind map:"

```
Reading-Tracker App
├── Analogy — habit mechanics
│   ├── One-tap logging — log in one tap so effort never blocks the habit
│   ├── Forgiving streaks — one missed day does not end the streak
│   └── Daily reminder — a nudge at a time the reader chooses
├── Combination — insight
│   ├── Weekly summary — one screen shows the week's reading
│   └── Reading statistics — trends over months
├── Analogy — community
│   ├── Book clubs — small groups read together
│   └── Progress sharing — share progress with friends
└── Inversion — content
    ├── Book discovery — find the next book
    └── Goal-setting onboarding — set a goal before the first log
```

User: "Habit mechanics and the weekly summary stand out. Book clubs feel heavy for an MVP."

**Confirmed candidates**: one-tap logging, forgiving streaks, daily reminder, weekly summary, goal-setting onboarding, book discovery.

### 5. evaluate-ideas
Facilitator: "Scoring the main candidates:"

| Idea | Value | Feasibility | Differentiation | Scope | Impact | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Habit core (logging + streaks) | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 | Retention is the goal; one-tap is simple; streaks are common |
| Weekly summary | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 1 | The retention hook; cheap to build; not unique |
| Goal-setting onboarding | 2 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | Sets the habit; needs design work; onboarding is standard |

Facilitator: "By effort vs impact, the habit core and weekly summary win; goal-setting onboarding follows. Book discovery and book clubs drop; the daily reminder is a small add we can decide later."

**Confirmed priorities**: habit core, weekly summary, goal-setting onboarding.

### 6. challenge-ideas
Facilitator: "Have you considered that strict streaks create pressure and drop-off when broken?"
User: "True — make streaks forgiving or optional."
Facilitator: "What if goal-setting onboarding matters more than streaks for retention?"
User: "Goal-setting drives the habit; streaks are a reward on top."
Facilitator: "Is the weekly summary worth its build effort for the MVP?"
User: "It is the retention hook — keep it simple."

**Stress-tested ideas**: forgiving streaks; goal-setting is core; weekly summary kept simple.

### 7. refine-idea
Facilitator: "What is essential versus nice-to-have?"
User: "Essential: one-tap logging, goal-setting onboarding, weekly summary. Nice-to-have: streaks, book discovery."
Facilitator: "Which remaining risk or gap does the idea still not cover?"
User: "Forgetting to open the app — a daily reminder covers that gap."

**Refined idea**: A mobile app for casual readers that pairs goal-setting onboarding with one-tap logging and a simple weekly summary, plus a daily reminder.

### 8. compile-actionable-plan
The facilitator compiles the plan per **plan-output-format**:

## Plan
A reading-habit app for casual readers: set a goal, log reading in one tap, and get a weekly summary plus a daily nudge.

## Why this plan
- Reaches G1 and G3 — the MVP is small enough to launch in 3 months and retain beta users.
- Removes C2 and C3 — the weekly summary and one-tap logging counter drop-off and logging friction.
- Leaves out book discovery and book clubs — both add scope before retention is proven.

## Goals
- **G1** Launch a usable product in 3 months — success: public beta live by month 3.
- **G2** Learn mobile development — success: ship the MVP without outside help.
- **G3** Close a beta with 20 weekly-active users — success: 20 weekly actives for two consecutive weeks.

## Decisions
- **D1** Chose one-tap logging — directly serves retention.
- **D2** Dropped forgiving streaks — nice-to-have; the weekly summary carries retention.
- **D3** Dropped book clubs — heavy for an MVP; revisit after launch.
- **D4** Dropped book discovery — large scope, medium value.
- **D5** Added daily reminder — covers the forgetting gap found in refine.
- **D6** Open: whether goal-setting onboarding or the weekly summary drives retention.

## Risks
- **C1** Weekend-only development time — impact: High — mitigation: strict MVP scope; iOS first — serves: G1.
- **C2** Retention drop-off — impact: High — mitigation: weekly summary + daily reminder — serves: G3.
- **C3** Logging friction — impact: High — mitigation: one-tap logging — serves: G3.
- **C4** No budget for paid services — impact: Medium — mitigation: self-hosted backend and open-source tools — serves: G1.

## Next steps
1. **S1** Define the MVP data model → produces `schema.md` — done when the user, book, and session models are reviewed — serves: G1.
2. **S2** Build one-tap logging → produces the logging screen — done when a book is logged in one tap — serves: G3, C3.
3. **S3** Build goal-setting onboarding → produces the onboarding flow — done when a new user sets a goal before the first log — serves: G1, G3.
4. **S4** Add the weekly summary → produces the summary screen — done when a week of logs renders on one screen — serves: G3, C2.
5. **S5** Add the daily reminder → produces a scheduled notification — done when a reminder fires at the chosen time — serves: G3, C2.
6. **S6** Learn the framework by shipping each screen → produces working native screens — done when no screen needs outside help — serves: G2.
7. **S7** Run the closed beta and measure weekly actives → produces a beta report — done when 20 users are active for two weeks — serves: G3.

Facilitator: "Here is the plan — it passes the four quality checks. Want to amend any section before we lock it?"
