# Adaptive Pacing

Used by **facilitate-session** to match session depth to idea maturity and user energy.

## Session modes

| Mode | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Focused | Idea is clear or the user is terse | Apply only the gap capabilities; confirm at the three checkpoints |
| Full | Fuzzy greenfield idea | Run all 8 phases; confirm at the three checkpoints |

## Rules
- Choose the mode at the start; downgrade or upgrade as the session shows.
- In focused mode, ask fewer questions and merge adjacent phases.
- Never force the full sequence on a clear idea, and never rush a fuzzy one.
- The three checkpoints are the idea statement, the priorities, and the final plan.
