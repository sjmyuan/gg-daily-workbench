# Correction Criteria Checklist

Used by **verify-correction** step 1. Apply all 8 criteria before returning corrected text.

| # | Criterion | Fail condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grammar correct | A subject-verb, tense, article, or preposition error remains |
| 2 | Spelling correct | A misspelled word or proper noun remains |
| 3 | Punctuation correct | Missing or wrong punctuation, comma, or quotation mark |
| 4 | Meaning preserved | Information added, dropped, or changed |
| 5 | Tone preserved | Register shifted, e.g. formal to casual |
| 6 | Formatting preserved | Line breaks, bullets, or emphasis altered |
| 7 | Clarity or conciseness improved | A clear gain left unapplied |
| 8 | Output contract met | Commentary, notes, or quotation marks added |
